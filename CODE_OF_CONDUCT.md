# Code of conduct

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith, and give them the
same benefit of the doubt you would want.

Concretely, in issues, pull requests, and reviews:

- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This misses the case where the
  directory is replaced mid-walk" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- **Say why.** A change request without a reason is an instruction; with one it
  is an argument someone can agree or disagree with.
- **Assume the other person knows something you do not.** Much of this code
  turns on platform behaviour that is genuinely surprising, and "that is
  wrong" is often "that is wrong on the one system I have."
- **Take no for an answer.** Not every proposal fits, and a maintainer
  declining one is not an insult.

Harassment, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not welcome, and
participation may be withdrawn from anyone engaging in them.

Report a problem to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately.
